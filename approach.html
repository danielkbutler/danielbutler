01—GEO / AEO

Four questions an AI answer engine is asking

Generative search reduces to what a model can confidently say about a business, and where it learned it. I plan GEO work around four questions.

  1. 01
    Entity clarity

    What is this business?

    Unambiguous signals about the category, services and locations, on the site and off it.

  2. 02
    Associations

    What is it known for?

    The topics and attributes third parties connect the business with.

  3. 03
    Proof

    Why should I recommend it?

    Independent evidence for the attributes that matter to the searcher.

  4. 04
    Citations

    Where did I learn that?

    A strong, varied footprint of sources a model can cite.

02—Presale

A presale process anyone can run

I wrote the agency's SEO presale process so the knowledge lives in the document, not in one person's head. It runs from account manager handoff to closed deal.

  1. 01
    Account managers

    Handoff

    Scope, geographies, site, GSC and GA4 access, and deal context: budget range, known competitors, why they're shopping.

  2. 02
    SEO

    QC and research

    Metadata review, content and local page gaps, PageSpeed, keyword research and competitive gap analysis, then priority keywords and geographies.

  3. 03
    SEO

    Quoting

    Hours calculated from a standard formula, plus analytics setup where the prospect needs it.

  4. 04
    SEO

    Deck build

    GSC data and research imported into Claude, output checked for accuracy, then a pitch deck with pricing.

  5. 05
    SEO

    Outcome

    Won or lost logged, and client lists and hours tracking updated on a win.

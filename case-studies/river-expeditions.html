01—The problem

Page-one rankings, but not for the searches that book trips

The core commercial page sat on page two for West Virginia rafting terms, and the homepage was competing with it across 20+ keywords. Broad searches were crowded by the map pack and an AI Overview. It's a small team, so every change had to earn its place.

02—What I did

How the work was sequenced

  1. 01
    Research

    Cross-checked four data sources

    Semrush positions, a keyword gap against three competing outfitters, GSC, and GA4. GSC settled disagreements, since Semrush ran 4–8 positions optimistic on this site.

  2. 02
    Prioritization

    Picked three bets a small team could ship

    A New River Gorge page rewrite, a Gauley River content cluster, and beginner and float trip pages. The New River Gorge page went first: it already had 8,000+ impressions and ranked 5–9 for part of its cluster.

  3. 03
    Hub cleanup

    Rebuilt the rafting hub

    Rewrote the title, H1, meta description, and intro, expanded the content, and updated anchor text so each search points to the page built for it. I also worked with the dev team to add a trip-type filter at the top of the page, so visitors find the right trip faster.

  4. 04
    Rewrite

    Rewrote the New River Gorge page

    Rewrote the metadata and expanded the content to target the commercial West Virginia rafting keywords.

01—The problem

A launch point and campsite that deserved a stronger page

Knights Eddy is one of the outfitter's three river bases: a launch point for rafting, tubing, and paddling trips, and a riverfront campsite. It serves several trip types and booking decisions, but its page drew just 147 sessions the prior summer.

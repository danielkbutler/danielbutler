01—The problem

A specialty service buried on page six

The practice's content for one of its specialty services averaged position 54. People were actively searching these topics, but the site rarely showed up. Owning them builds the topical authority that supports every related service page.

02—What I did

How the work was sequenced

  1. 01
    Research

    Found the gaps competitors were filling

    Keyword and competitor research showed steady search demand for the service, and competitors ranking for topics the site didn't cover at all.

  2. 02
    Build

    Built the specialty service content

    I built every page: the content, the structure, and the internal links connecting the new pages to each other and to the practice's service pages.

  3. 03
    Approach

    Wrote for direct answers

    Each page answers a specific patient question plainly and early, with clear signals about who the practice is and what it treats. It's the kind of content AI answer engines pull from.

Showing up in Google's AI Overviews

The content I built is now cited as a source in Google's AI Overviews, so the practice appears in the answer itself, above the traditional results. That comes from how the pages were written: each one states clearly what the practice treats, answers the patient's question directly, and backs it up.

It's the same approach behind my GEO/AEO framework, which I now use to plan AI search work across client accounts.

01—The problem

Parents were searching for help, not program names

All for Kids helps parents get paid to care for their own children through Colorado Medicaid programs like IHSS and CFC. Most parents who qualify don't know those acronyms yet. The site was written for the ones who already did.

02—What I did

How the work was sequenced

  1. 01
    Research

    Found two audiences in the data

    GSC showed program-aware searchers. Semrush showed problem-aware parents describing their situation in their own words. Same need, different vocabulary.

  2. 02
    Strategy

    Led with the broadest entry point

    Chose “in home care for children with special needs” as the primary keyword for the core page, with IHSS and CFC as supporting references instead of the headline.

  3. 03
    Build

    Built a dedicated IHSS page

    For parents ready for program detail, linked from the broader page. “denver ihss” went from not ranking to position 5.

  4. 04
    Build

    Built out program and location pages

    Added PASA services, Lakewood IHSS, and family caregiver pages, plus a special needs page, so each program and location parents search for has a page built for it.
